# MONSOON-GUARD: Operational Limitations & Scientific Boundaries

To uphold scientific integrity and public safety in agro-meteorological advisory systems, the following operational constraints and limitations are explicitly declared:

---

## 1. Subseasonal Predictability Barrier
- **Atmospheric Chaos:** Deterministic predictability of convective precipitation in the tropics diminishes rapidly beyond 5 to 7 days.
- **Probabilistic Spread:** Subseasonal forecasts at 14, 21, and 30 days rely on slowly evolving boundary conditions (SSTs, soil moisture memory, ENSO, IOD, MJO). Consequently, sharp probability spikes (e.g. $> 85\%$) at Day 30 are rare and should be interpreted with wider error bands.
- **Skill Horizon:** Heavy convective rainfall ($\ge 64.5\text{ mm/day}$) has limited predictability at 21–30 days compared to regional synoptic monsoon breaks. Skill scores drop significantly beyond Day 14 for localized extremes.

---

## 2. Downscaling and Spatial Representation
- **Topographic Complexities:** Statistical downscaling from ~25 km ERA5 / ~10 km IMERG to block centroids captures macro-orographic effects (e.g., Bastar plateau elevation) but cannot resolve hyper-microclimatic valley winds or isolated cloudburst cells ($< 5\text{ km}$).
- **Station Network Density:** In regions with sparse automated weather station (AWS) ground truth, satellite retrieval errors in satellite radar (IMERG) may propagate into training targets.

---

## 3. Data Latency & Fallback Modes
- **Real-Time Latency:** ERA5 final reanalysis has an approximate 5-day latency, while ERA5T (preliminary) has ~1 day. Real-time operations require ERA5T / IMERG Early run data.
- **Synthetic Data Mode (`DATA_MODE=synthetic`):** When running in an offline environment without CDS/Earthdata API credentials, MONSOON-GUARD generates synthetic data statistically matching the real distributions. Whenever synthetic data is active, the UI displays an unmissable **SYNTHETIC DATA BANNER** and all API responses declare `"data_source": "synthetic"`.

---

## 4. Explainability & Non-Causality
- **Correlation vs Causality:** SHAP values generated by tree models represent statistical sensitivity within the trained feature space. They do **not** establish thermodynamic causality. For instance, a low surface pressure SHAP attribution reflects co-occurrence with onset, not that pressure alone induced rainfall.

---

## 5. Agronomic Advisory Governance
- **Statutory Authority:** MONSOON-GUARD advisories are decision-support aids for field officers and farmers. Official warnings and statutory agricultural contingency advisories remain under the prerogative of IMD Agromet / State Agricultural Departments / ICAR-CRIDA.
- **KVK Threshold Calibration:** All crop moisture thresholds and dry-spell tolerances in `config/crops.yaml` are initial agronomic models that must be tuned with local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) field trials before commercial sowing guidance is finalized.
